Automated chemical dosing for a powder coating wash line

Why it mattered

Clean, correctly dosed wash tanks are key to coating quality. Chemicals were added by hand from totes, which took labour and created spill, exposure and dosing error risks. Rinse water also drains back into the tanks and slowly contaminates the solution.

What I did

  • Co-created the program and timeline for a chemical supplier change, sent parts out for testing, and finalized the new wash line chemistries.
  • Installed an automated dosing system with the vendor, maintenance and the Chem-tech, including setpoints, alarms and SOPs.
  • Drew the AutoCAD P&ID for phase 2: automatic tank draining and filling.

How it works

  • A conductivity meter in the tank signals the controller, which runs the dosing pump to keep the chemical at its setpoint.
  • When the spray pump runs, solenoid valves drain 1 GPM and add 1 GPM of city water, so the level stays constant and the solution stays clean.

P-1 spray pump, P-2 dosing pump, S solenoid valve, FR flow regulator, FM flowmeter, LM level meter, CM conductivity meter.

Results

  • Saves $80K a year and removes manual dosing and operator chemical exposure.
  • One person can now cover both Chem-tech and QC tasks, saving about 1 hour per shift.
  • Automatic draining and filling is expected to save another hour per shift and remove end-of-day and stage 4 drains.

A 5S hook rack that cuts burn-off oven loads from four to one

Chemical Engineering Intern, Mississauga

Tangled hooks in wire baskets were slowing loaders down and blocking oven airflow. I ran a time study, sized a 5S rack in Excel to hold 2,520 hooks, and built two prototypes with maintenance.

From problem to prototype

  1. Problem: hooks tangled in baskets were slow to find and blocked oven airflow.
  2. Constraint: designed around the existing burn-off oven rack. Cycles took 6–8 h.
  3. Design: hand sketch for maintenance, 36 × 45 × 88 in.
  4. Prototype 1: rows at 6 in spacing were too close together.
  5. Prototype 2: spacing increased to 8 in and rows raised for easier reach.

Design decisions

  • Counted all 620 hooks with two coworkers and sized the rack in Excel to hold 2,520.
  • Kept rows near the middle to limit bending and overreaching.
  • Used a rack instead of baskets for fewer contact points and better oven airflow.

Results and next steps

  • Tripled hook storage, so one burn-off cycle replaces four.
  • Oven cycles are expected to drop from about 7 h to 3–5 h. Results were pending at the end of my term.
  • Greensolv can be eliminated, saving $300–700 a month plus heating and refill costs.
  • Next: a trial with loaders and unloaders, then more racks built to the same dimensions.

Time study

I ran a PDCA time study of loaders, unloaders and packagers on both lines, separating value-added from non-value-added time. Loaders spent about 14% and 9% of their time looking for hooks, which drove the move away from baskets. Unloading was only 15.6% and 9.7% value-added. Most of the rest was waiting.

Steam generator TSP voltage analysis

Why
Tube support plate voltage data from eddy current inspections tracks degradation across 8 boilers, and the analysis was done by hand in Excel.
How
Designed and automated 4 statistical analyses in Python, including voltage change between inspections, proximity maps that compare each tube with its neighbours, and CDFs by calibration group.
Result
Cut total analysis time by 85% and flagged locally unusual readings for engineering review.

Thermal assessment of 48 steam generators

Why
Cooling requirements were needed to size HVAC equipment and plan plant additions.
How
Applied heat transfer principles to thermally assess all 48 steam generators.
Result
Established cooling requirements that informed HVAC sizing, capacity planning and plant additions.

Operating experience (OPEX) failure analysis

Why
Lifecycle cost and reliability planning need evidence of how parts actually fail.
How
Analyzed 500+ valve and E&IC failures through OPEX research and identified root causes and corrective actions.

Supported lifecycle cost estimates and reliability planning for a nuclear plant line.
